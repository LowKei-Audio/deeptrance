DeepTrance User Guide

Version 1.0.0 · VST3 and standalone for Windows · by LowKei

DeepTrance turns static pads, plucks, leads and vocals into moving, euphoric trance
textures. It combines rhythmic gating, swirling modulation, vowel filtering, tape drift,
acid grit, granular clouds, fractal echoes and an infinite reverb in one plug-in, with
performance macros you can ride live or automate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Installing

1.  Run DeepTrance-1.0.0-Windows-Setup.exe.
2.  Choose VST3 plug-in and standalone app, or VST3 plug-in only.
3.  Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins:
    -   Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders >
        Rescan.
    -   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins.
    -   Serato Studio: Settings > Plugins > enable VST3 > Rescan.

The VST3 is installed to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\DeepTrance.vst3, the standard
folder every DAW scans. The standalone app appears in the Start menu as DeepTrance.

  Windows may show a SmartScreen notice the first time you run the installer. Choose More
  info > Run anyway.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Quick Start

1.  Put DeepTrance on a pad, pluck or vocal track.
2.  Press play. DeepTrance follows your project tempo automatically.
3.  Drag the dot in the Deep Space pad: right adds swirl, up adds space.
4.  Click GATE in the module strip for an instant trance rhythm.
5.  Step through presets with the < and > arrows at the top.

By default DeepTrance loads with a gentle phaser (Swirl), subtle tape width (Drift), a
dotted-eighth ping-pong echo and a medium reverb, so it sounds musical from the first
second.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. The Two Views

Click EASY or PRO in the top-right corner. Switching never changes the sound, and your
choice is saved with your project.

Easy view

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Control                                      What it does
  -------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------
  Deep Space pad                               X = Swirl intensity, Y = Space (delay and
                                               reverb). Double-click to recentre.

  GATE                                         How hard the Trance Gate chops (when the
                                               gate is on).

  SWIRL                                        Phaser/flanger depth and mix.

  SPACE                                        Delay and reverb amount, plus reverb size.

  DRIFT                                        Tape wobble and width.

  BUILD-UP                                     The tension knob for breakdowns (see section
                                               5).

  MIX                                          Overall dry/wet.

  COMBO + Amount                               One-click effect chains (see section 6).

  Gate Pattern                                 Choose the rhythm without opening Pro.

  Module strip                                 One click turns each module on or off.

  FREEZE CLOUD / FREEZE ABYSS                  Hold the current sound indefinitely.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The four intensity macros sit at 50% by default, which means “exactly as set in Pro”. Turn
one down to 0% to remove that effect, or up to 100% to double it.

Pro view

Every module has its own card with an ON switch in its header. Scroll to reach the
Modulation section at the bottom. Drag a knob to change it; double-click to return it to
its default. The mouse wheel scrolls the page and never changes a knob.

4. The Modules

DeepTrance processes in this order:

Gate > Vowel > Swirl > Drift > Acid > Build-Up > Punch > Cloud > Delay > Abyss > Mix

Trance Gate (Rhythm Enforcer)

A tempo-synced, 16-step volume and filter gate.

-   Rate: 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 or triplets.
-   Pattern: eight factory rhythms (Classic 16ths, Offbeat Pump, Driving, Gallop, Stutter,
    Accent Wave, Half Time, Build Roll) or Custom.
-   Draw your own: in Pro, draw on the 16-step grid. Drawing over a factory pattern copies
    it into Custom first, so every pattern is an editable starting point.
-   Depth, Length, Attack, Release: how deep, how long and how smooth each step is.
-   Filter: closed steps also sweep a low-pass filter down, for a softer “breathing” gate.

Vowel Filter (The Voice)

Morphs through A-E-I-O-U formants for talking leads. LFO Depth and LFO Rate sweep the
vowel in time with your track. Resonance sharpens the formants.

Swirl (Dual-Band Phase and Flange)

Phaser, flanger or both, synced to tempo. Only the highs swirl; the bass below the
Crossover stays solid. Raise Low Band to let some of the lows move too.

Drift (Wow and Flutter)

Slow, tape-like pitch wobble for the wide, slightly detuned sound of classic 90s anthems.
Amount reaches about 25 cents at maximum.

Acid (Aggressive Saturation)

A resonant 303-style filter with an accent envelope (the “squelch”), followed by
three-band distortion. Drive is level-matched, so turning it up changes the character, not
the volume.

Punch (Psy-Transient Shaper)

Sharpens the attack of plucks and kicks so they cut through a wet mix, and briefly ducks
the delay, reverb and cloud after each hit (Duck and Duck Release).

Cloud (Granular Engine)

Smears the live input into a cloud of tiny grains.

-   Size, Density: short, sparse grains stutter; long, dense grains become a pad.
-   Position, Spray: how far back grains reach, and how scattered they are.
-   Pitch, Jitter, Shimmer: transpose, detune each grain, or throw grains up an octave.
-   Freeze: stops listening and loops the current moment, perfect before a drop.

Fractal Delay (Ping-Pong)

A stereo echo whose feedback runs through its own pitch shifter and filter. Set Pitch to
+12 for crystalline, climbing echoes, or below zero for a deep, murky wash. Tone darkens
each repeat. Sync off lets you set the time in milliseconds.

Abyss Reverb (Infinite Space)

A lush, modulated reverb with decays up to 60 seconds. Shimmer adds octave-up sparkle.
Freeze holds the tail indefinitely, turning a short pluck into an endless drone.

  Delay, Cloud and Abyss tails ring out naturally when you switch them off.

5. Build-Up (Tension Generator)

Automate BUILD-UP from 0% to 100% over 8 or 16 bars before a drop. As it rises,
DeepTrance:

-   sweeps a steep high-pass filter upward, removing the bass,
-   adds a white-noise riser that follows the sweep (Build-Up Noise sets its level),
-   increases the reverb amount, size and decay,
-   speeds up the Swirl.

Snap it back to 0% on the drop.

6. Combos

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Combo                                        What happens
  -------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------
  Astral Projection                            Removes the dry signal, routes a granular
                                               smear into a huge shimmer reverb, and sweeps
                                               a slow 8-bar low-pass over it. Turns a fast
                                               arpeggio into an instant ambient breakdown.

  Acid Bath                                    Engages the resonant 303 filter and
                                               distortion with a fast 1/16 ping-pong delay.
                                               Turns a clean pluck into a gritty acid line.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Amount blends from your own settings (0%) to the full combo (100%). Automate it to morph
into a combo over a few bars.

7. Modulation (Pro view)

LFO 1 and LFO 2

-   Shapes: Sine, Triangle, Saw Up, Saw Down, Square, Sample & Hold, Smooth Random and
    Custom.
-   Custom: draw your own 16-point shape in the editor under each LFO. Drawing switches
    the LFO to Custom automatically. Your drawing is saved with the project and with user
    presets.
-   Sync: on = locked to the bar (1/32 to 16 bars); off = free rate in Hz.

Envelope Follower

Follows the input level. Sensitivity lifts quiet sources so they still drive the
modulation.

Mod Matrix

Eight slots. Each slot connects a source (LFO 1, LFO 2 or Envelope) to a destination, with
a positive or negative amount.

Destinations include gate depth and filter, vowel, swirl depth/feedback/mix, drift, acid
cutoff/resonance/drive, cloud position/size/density/pitch/jitter/mix, delay
feedback/pitch/tone/mix, abyss size/decay/shimmer/mix, punch attack, Build-Up and the
global mix.

  Try: LFO 1, Saw Up, 8 Bars > Cloud Position at 40% for a pad that slowly scans through a
  vocal.

8. Presets

Use < and > to step through presets, or click the preset name for the full menu.

Factory presets (26):

-   Pads: Euphoric Wash, Gated Supersaw, Talking Pad, Frozen Horizon
-   Plucks: Classic 3/16 Echo, Crystal Ascend, Murky Descent, Psy Punch
-   Leads: Acid Squelch, Vowel Lead, 90s Anthem, Flange Rider
-   Vocals: Breathe Cloud, Angel Shimmer, Stutter Chop
-   Build-Ups: 8-Bar Riser, Tension Gate, Infinite Riser
-   FX: Astral Projection, Acid Bath, Frozen Abyss, Glitch Tops, Wobble Space, Breathing
    Swirl, Pumping Space

Your presets: click SAVE and give it a name. User presets are stored in
Documents\DeepTrance Presets as .dtpreset files, so you can back them up or share them.
The menu also has Open Presets Folder.

The factory presets also appear in your DAW’s own preset list.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. Tips for Each DAW

-   Ableton Live: use Configure on the device title bar to expose macros for Push or clip
    automation.
-   FL Studio: right-click any knob > Create automation clip. Build-Up is perfect for
    this.
-   Serato Studio: load DeepTrance as a channel FX. The Easy view keeps everything one
    click away during a live set.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10. Performance

DeepTrance adds no latency, so it stays in time in every DAW. Effects that are switched
off use no CPU once their tails finish.

Measured CPU use (48 kHz, one core):

  Setup                                     CPU
  ----------------------------------------- -----------
  Default sound                             about 3%
  Every module on, 8 mod slots active       about 6%
  Worst case (96 dense grains + Build-Up)   about 13%

11. Troubleshooting

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Problem                                      Fix
  -------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------
  DeepTrance does not appear in the DAW        Rescan plug-ins (section 1). Make sure your
                                               DAW scans
                                               C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

  The gate is out of time                      DeepTrance follows the DAW tempo. With the
                                               transport stopped it keeps running at the
                                               last tempo; press play to lock it to the
                                               grid.

  A frozen sound will not stop                 Click FREEZE ABYSS or FREEZE CLOUD again, or
                                               switch that module off.

  Too much low end in the reverb               Raise Abyss Damping, or use Punch to duck
                                               the tail.

  The standalone app makes no sound            Click Options > Audio/MIDI Settings and
                                               choose your audio device and input.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

12. Specifications

-   Format: VST3 (64-bit) and standalone, Windows 10/11
-   Supported hosts: Ableton Live, FL Studio, Serato Studio
-   Channels: stereo or mono
-   Sample rates: 44.1 to 192 kHz
-   Latency: 0 samples
-   Parameters: 133, all automatable
-   Validation: Steinberg VST3 validator (47/47) and pluginval at strictness level 10
